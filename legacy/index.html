FAQ

AI video dubbing questions

How Glotix works as a dubbing website for creators who need the original mix to stay.

What is the best AI video dubbing tool that keeps the original music?

Glotix isolates dialogue from the soundtrack, translates the script, synthesizes new speech with optional voice cloning, and mixes it back over the original music and effects.

How does Glotix compare to other dubbing websites?

Glotix is built for dialogue replacement, not a full mix replacement. Upload a clip, pick a language, clone the original speaker if you want, and download a dubbed file with the instrumental bed intact.

Does Glotix include text to speech?

Yes. Text to Speech uses the same public voice catalog as Dubbing Studio, including any cloned voices saved to your account.
