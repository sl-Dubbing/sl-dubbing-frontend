What AI dubbing does

Dubbing replaces spoken lines in a video with speech in another language while keeping picture timing. Glotix runs that path with AI: transcription, translation, synthesis, and a remix over the original bed. Use it as an AI video translator when you need a downloadable dubbed file, not only burned-in subtitles.

Keep original music and effects

Many “AI video translator” tools replace or mute the whole mix. Glotix splits dialogue from music, then puts the new voice back on the same instrumental. That is the difference if you searched for AI dubbing and also care about the song or ambience already in the clip.

AI music vs soundtrack preservation

Search results for “AI music” often mean tools that compose new tracks. Glotix does not compose music. If your query is dubbing plus music, the product promise is: keep the music you already have, replace only the spoken track.

AI dubbing FAQ

What is AI dubbing?

AI dubbing replaces spoken dialogue with speech in another language. Glotix transcribes, translates, synthesizes, and mixes the new vocal over the original music and effects.

Can AI dubbing keep the original music?

Yes. Dialogue is separated from the instrumental bed, then the new voice is remixed over that bed. The soundtrack is not generated from scratch.

Is Glotix an AI music generator?

No. It is an AI video dubbing and text-to-speech studio. Music already in your file is preserved.
